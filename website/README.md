# Xavcomm'27 — Tapestry of Stories
### Official Festival & Sponsorship Web Portal
**Department of Mass Communication, St. Xavier's University, Kolkata**  
*In association with St. Xavier's University Kolkata Alumni Association*

---

## 🌟 Overview
This modern web portal has been crafted for **Xavcomm'27**, the 5th edition of Eastern India's flagship inter-college media festival taking place on **29th and 30th January, 2027**.

All content, pricing tiers, deliverables matrix, past event highlights, previous corporate sponsors, and official contact convenors are strictly curated from the official **Xavcomm'27 Sponsorship Brochure**.

## ✨ Features Included
1. **Hero Section & Live Countdown**:
   - Live real-time countdown timer to January 29, 2027.
   - Dual Call-to-Actions for sponsorship exploration and team contact.
   - Key statistics counters (2500+ daily footfall, 30+ colleges, 12+ domains, 5th edition).

2. **SXUK & Mass Communication Department Showcase**:
   - History and legacy of St. Xavier's University, Kolkata (17-acre campus at Rajarhat, SXC Vision 2020).
   - Advanced facilities highlight: multi-camera production studios, teleprompters, sound labs, and high-end Apple Mac post-production suites.

3. **Festival Theme: "Tapestry of Stories"**:
   - Deep dive into Journalism, Film & Video Production, Advertising & PR Strategy, and Photography & Digital Art.

4. **Hall of Fame & Past Stars**:
   - Features Dr. Shashi Tharoor, Shreya Jain, Asees Kaur, Yellow Taxi Band, and Tatrix.
